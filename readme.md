# Startup Sound

A 2D multiplayer RPG made with Lua and LÖVE 2D.

## What is Startup Sound?

Startup Sound is a retro-style multiplayer game about an old computer, a mysterious virus, and two friends who end up trapped inside the computer.
The game takes inspiration from old PC games, Windows 95, pixel art and classic RPGs.
The idea is pretty simple:
You are playing a game on an old Windows 95 computer.
Then you notice something strange.
There is a virus on the computer.
You try to get rid of it.
Instead, something goes completely wrong.
You get pulled into the screen.
When you wake up, you're inside the computer.
But the computer isn't the way you remember it.
The desktop has turned into a huge RPG-like world, files have become places, programs have become characters, and the viruses are now actual enemies.
And then you find out that your friend has been pulled in too.
Now you have to explore the computer together and find a way back home.

## The Story

The game starts with a normal kid playing retro games on his old PC.
Everything is fine until strange things start happening.
Programs behave differently.
Files appear where they shouldn't.
The computer starts glitching.
Eventually, the player discovers that the system is infected by a virus.
Thinking it will be easy to remove, he tries to delete it.
Instead, the computer reacts.
The screen glitches and pulls him inside.
He wakes up in a completely different world.
It looks like a computer, but it works like an RPG.
The deeper he goes, the more corrupted the world becomes.
He eventually finds his friend, who was pulled into the computer as well.
Together, they have to explore the system, fight the viruses and figure out what caused everything.

## Gameplay

Startup Sound is focused around exploration, combat and playing through the story with another player.
Players will explore different parts of the computer, fight enemies and discover what is happening to the system.
The world is built around computer-related ideas.
Folders can become buildings.
Files can become items.
Programs can become NPCs.
Corrupted data can become dangerous areas.
Viruses become enemies and bosses.
The further you get into the computer, the stranger everything becomes.

## Multiplayer

Multiplayer is one of the main parts of the game.
The game is designed around two players exploring the world together.
Players can fight enemies, explore areas and take on bosses as a team.
The multiplayer system is still being developed, so this part of the game may change during development.

## Bosses

The main enemies are different types of computer viruses.
The goal is for each major boss to feel different instead of just being a stronger normal enemy.
Some bosses may control entire areas of the computer, while others may be connected to important parts of the story.

## The World

The computer itself is basically the game's RPG world.
You may start in familiar places like the desktop, but eventually you'll reach areas that are much more corrupted and strange.
Some planned areas include:

* The Desktop
* Recycle Bin
* Program Files
* System
* Virus Core
  These are not final and may change as the game develops.

## Style

The game uses a retro pixel-art style inspired by old computers and games.
The visual style will take ideas from Windows 95 without trying to recreate it exactly.
The goal is to make the game feel like an old computer that somehow turned into an RPG.

## Why Startup Sound?

The name comes from the startup sound of an old computer.
That familiar sound is basically where the whole idea starts.
You turn on an old PC expecting to play a game.
Instead, the computer becomes the game.
